    Make a Movie Battles 2 map in three steps

    An example map is already loaded so you can try everything right away.

    1. Build: pick Floor and drag on the grid to make rooms. Pick a look, a ceiling height, or open sky first. Wall removes floor.
    2. Teams: click Rebels or Imperials, then click the map to place where each team starts. Or press Place for me.
    3. Play: press Download playable map. Use Walk around any time to explore it first.

    Your map is ready

    1. Open the downloaded map.zip.
    2. Drag map.pk3 into your GameData › MBII folder. On Steam that is Steam\steamapps\common\Jedi Academy\GameData\MBII.
    3. Start Movie Battles II, open the console with ~ and type /map mb2_map. Or host a game and pick it from the map list.
